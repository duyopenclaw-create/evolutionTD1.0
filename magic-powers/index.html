How To Play

🧙 Move with WASD/Arrows or the left joystick. Each segment is a real place — a chain of rooms, bridges, ramps and jump-gaps, not one flat arena. Explore it.

⬆️ Jump with Space / the jump button to clear gaps and climb ledges. Fall into a chasm and you'll respawn on the last safe ledge with a bit of fall damage — it's not fatal, just careful.

🏃 Hold Shift to sprint (drains stamina, regenerates when you stop).

⚔️ Attack with F / Enter / Left-Click / the red button — auto-swings at the nearest foe.

🔥 Cast spells with number keys 1-6 or the spell buttons once an element is unlocked in your Book (B).

🎒 Open your Backpack (I) to equip 2 weapons, a shield and armor found in chests.

📖 Every segment clear grants 1 Magic Point. Spend Magic Points in the Book to unlock/level elements up to level 100 — every 5th level is a big milestone upgrade and costs 2 points instead of 1. You always have a double-jump — leveling Wind just makes it stronger.

💰 Coins drop from monsters & chests. Spend them in the Shop on gear, more Magic Points (1000c) or Backpack slots (1000c/slot).

📦 Wooden chests: 10-100 coins. 🟡 Golden chests: 1-2 Magic Points + 50-250 coins. 💎 Diamond chests: 3-8 Magic Points + 100-500 coins. Some sit on hidden side-paths off the main route.

☠️ Watch for environmental hazards — lava, riptides, charged floors — that telegraph before they hurt. Some gaps have a moving platform shuttling across them — time your jump on and off. Some segments hide a 2-5 story tower to climb for a guaranteed diamond chest — some floors are open ledges needing real jumps (and double-jumps), and every floor also has a ladder: walk up to it and hold X (or the 🪜 button) to climb straight up or down, no jumping required. Kill everything in a segment, then find and reach the glowing portal to move on.

👹 Monsters now roam the whole level and will hunt you down wherever you are — expect more of them, but each one is weaker than before. Watch their health bars.

👹 Every 10th segment is a Boss. Every 10 chapters you enter a new elemental Realm. Beat Chapter 60's boss to win.
