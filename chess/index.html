HOW TO PLAY

♟
Goal

Checkmate the opponent's king — put it under attack with no escape.

🔄
Turns

White moves first. Players alternate turns, one piece per turn.

⚔
Capture

Move onto an opponent's square to capture their piece and remove it.

🏰
Castling

King moves 2 squares toward a rook; rook jumps to the other side. Neither piece can have moved before.

👻
En Passant

If a pawn advances 2 squares past an adjacent enemy pawn, that enemy pawn may capture it as if it moved only 1 square — but only immediately after.

♛
Promotion

When your pawn reaches the far rank, promote it to a queen, rook, bishop, or knight.

½
Draw

Stalemate (no legal moves but not in check) or 50 moves without a capture/pawn move.

⚠
Check

Your king is in check if any opponent piece attacks it. You must escape check immediately.

🖱
Select & Move

Click a piece to select it (blue highlight + dots show legal moves). Click a dot or highlighted square to move there.

⌨
Keyboard

Arrow keys navigate the board. Enter or Space to select/move. Escape to deselect.

↔
Review Mode

After the game ends, press "Review Game" to replay all moves. Use ← → arrow keys or the Prev/Next buttons to step through.

🎯
Best Move

In review, green highlights show what the engine considers the best move from each position.

↗
Arrows

Right-click and drag on the board to draw analysis arrows. Left-click clears them. Hold Shift/Alt/Ctrl for different colors.

🏆
Achievements

Earn achievements by playing. Each unlocks a visual reward — board themes, piece effects, and special animations.

🎵
Music & SFX

Toggle background music (Bach Prelude) and sound effects independently via the Controls panel.

🔍
Move Badges

Each move gets a quality badge: Brilliant (!), Book (📖), Forced (→), OK (✓), Mistake (?), Blunder (??).
