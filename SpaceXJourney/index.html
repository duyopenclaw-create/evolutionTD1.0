EARTH IS GONE. HUMANITY MUST SURVIVE.

// MISSION BRIEFING

EARTH YEAR 2087. Without warning, an alien armada emerged from deep space. Their gravity implosion device compressed Earth's core until the planet detonated. The explosion was visible from Mars.

You are Commander Alex Chen, one of 47 survivors aboard the SpaceX Ark I. Your suit holds 250% oxygen capacity. The hull absorbs 1000 damage before failure.

Asteroids rise from the debris belt behind you — 80 hull damage each. Alien-launched comets pierce the void ahead — 120 damage. Use food to repair the hull: 5 food = +500 HP.

You begin with 250% oxygen. Collect food to heal yourself and restore hunger. Press F to eat (1 food = +15 HP +30% hunger). At zero hunger you starve. Land on planets to find supplies.
